ShineBoard App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

Thank you for using ShineBoard. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ShineBoard handles information related to your use of the app.

1. Information We Collect

ShineBoard is designed primarily for local use on your device. Unless you contact us directly, we do not require you to create an account and do not intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment information.

To provide app features, ShineBoard may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Board content and presets: Text, colors, brightness, backgrounds, handwriting, and display settings you create.
  • Camera picture-in-picture preview: When you choose to use camera-related features, the app may request camera access and display a preview on your device.
  • Photo library: When you choose an image background or save your work, the app may request permission to read from or write to your photo library.
  • Biometric authentication: If you enable related protection features, the system may use Face ID, Touch ID, or device biometric capabilities for local authentication. ShineBoard does not receive or store your biometric data.
  • Local database: Some settings and presets may be stored locally on your device so you can continue using them later.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide core features such as light boards, fill light, scrolling text, backgrounds, and presets;
  • Save your local settings and improve your next use experience;
  • Respond to your questions and troubleshoot issues when you request technical support;
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, or platform requirements.

3. Storage

ShineBoard stores its main data locally on your device. You may delete local data through app features, system settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling the app generally removes local data stored with the app, although exact behavior may vary by operating system version.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent;
  • To comply with laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful government requests;
  • To protect the rights, property, and safety of users, us, or the public.

5. App Permissions

You can manage or disable app permissions at any time in your system settings. If certain permissions are disabled, related features may not work properly.

  • Camera permission: Used for camera picture-in-picture preview.
  • Photo permission: Used to choose image backgrounds or save board creations.
  • Biometric permission: Used for local authentication, not for collecting biometric information.

6. Children's Privacy

ShineBoard is not specifically designed for children. If you are a minor, please use the app with the consent and guidance of a parent or legal guardian.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page or notify you by other appropriate means. Continued use of ShineBoard means you acknowledge the updated policy.
